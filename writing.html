Books

In progress · Saltmarsh Press

The Neurotic Gene

How seven remarkable lives, and one ordinary one, show that the feeling of inadequacy is installed and not inherent

A biographical and evolutionary argument that neurosis, the persistent unearned sense of not being enough, is not a personality trait but a product of the environments that shape us. Told through eight lives, including Virginia Woolf, Nikola Tesla and Charles Darwin, the book maps the conditions that install inadequacy and the conditions that don’t.

In progress · Saltmarsh Press

Useful

Why the Software Meant to Help You Is Making Everything Worse

Enterprise software is a design disaster, and most of the people who use it know it. This book asks why software that was built to help people work has become such an obstacle to getting anything done, and what it would take to fix it. Drawing on years of enterprise UX practice at Capgemini and beyond, it makes the case that usability is not a feature: it is the product.
