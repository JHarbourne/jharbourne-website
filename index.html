Founder · Platform owner · Community lead · AI tooling

Nearmark

Open-source · GPS · Heritage · iOS · Android · White-label

An open-source platform that gives any walkable place its own location-aware app—a heritage trail, an arts walk, a pub crawl. Built on Vue 3, Supabase and Leaflet. EU-hosted, no API key billing. Currently live as the Tollesbury Arts Trail and LGBT Heritage App.

Beyond the Work

I started in design as an art director at Chronos Publishing—publishers of the Pink Paper and Boyz—before the web existed as a profession. Sailor. Choir singer. Painter and photographer. Founder and chair of London’s bid to host the Gay Games at the Olympic Park. Founder of the London Raiders softball club (one of the largest in the world), Sport London, the London Gay Bikers, and the UK LGBT Archive. The through-line is the same as the work: bring people together, and make things that outlast you. Photography stocked on Alamy, Adobe Stock and Getty. Paintings and other work on Instagram.
