I design and build digital tools that communities need but the market won’t make: accessibility auditors, LGBTQ+ knowledge platforms, sailing club infrastructure. After two decades in UX and service design, I now work independently at the intersection of inclusive design and AI, turning strategy into shipped products.

Alongside that: apps reaching 50 million people, conference papers on AI and cultural heritage, writing published by Towards AI and the British Psychological Society, and a habit of founding communities and platforms that outlast everything else.

50M Visitors

LGBT History Project

Founder · Platform owner · Community lead · Agentic AI

A national LGBTQ+ heritage archive built and maintained by volunteers. 50 million visitors. Recognised with a Prime Minister’s Points of Light Award. Now home to Nearmark, a GPS-based heritage walking tour platform.

2 Live instances

Nearmark

Open-source · GPS · Heritage · iOS · Android · White-label

An open-source platform that gives any walkable place its own location-aware app: a heritage trail, an arts walk, a pub crawl. Built on Vue 3, Supabase and Leaflet. EU-hosted, no API key billing. Currently live as the Tollesbury Arts Trail and LGBT Heritage App.
